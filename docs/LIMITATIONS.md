# Limitations

The pinned offline candidate has 272 passing pytest cases (155 unit, 112 CLI/control, five browser), a fresh Vite 6.4.5 build, an npm audit snapshot with zero reported vulnerabilities, and two public-development evaluation checks reproduced locally and in an isolated Docker image. See [validation](VALIDATION.md) for exact source identity, count definitions and reproduction limits. This is bounded offline evidence, not completed voice evaluation or independent product acceptance.

- **Synthetic inputs:** V and C mocks use typed debug stimulus and parser-driven fixtures; K mock returns diagnostic tone. Fake microphone devices and generated tones do not establish speech recognition, real speakers, spoken-answer correctness or physical acoustic latency. The 112 CLI/control cases include 108 reporter controls and four arm-journey/trial-plan cases, not 112 spoken tasks. Fixture rows and repeated Docker checks are not extra test cases.
- **Browser scope:** Five built-UI Chromium cases cover startup, protected metadata POSTs, a mock journey, interruption/stale fencing and separate-origin refusal. They do not establish every resampling rate, acoustic barge-in/noise condition, device loss, reconnect schedule, queue/handle bound or platform. The same-origin test composition is not production static hosting. The provisional energy-window/resampler choices still need physical-device validation.
- **Timing, calibration and soak:** Synthetic calibration arithmetic is exercised. Full offline soak, live soak and hardware calibration were not executed. No observed speech-end-to-acceptable-audio latency, physical sound-at-ear measurement, resource-growth guarantee or architecture winner is claimed. Missing platform/heap/GC metrics remain unknown. Attempted actions do not prove admitted/terminal coverage or post-End cleanup.
- **Live providers and bills:** No provider call, live budget, supported replacement TTS decision, current tariff certification or paid comparison is established. Guarded native/STT/TTS transport source and synthetic wire tests do not certify real endpoint/model/voice/cancel/usage compatibility. Reserve-before-call and retained unknown liability constrain local admission under their assumptions; they do not certify an external bill. Live booking confirmation still needs a genuinely trusted independent input-confirmation verifier.
- **Reports and denominators:** Inline fixture checks exercise missing terminals, censored ranks, unknown costs and byte-identical reproduction. They do not establish an independent speech/task oracle or real saved-artifact provenance. Missing/partial assignments, unresolved starts and reservation-only ambiguity must continue to withhold complete all-started headlines. Paired intervals remain conditional when pairs are missing. Raw receipt/render estimates and correctness-qualified timing are distinct.
- **Access, rights and custody:** Saved-artifact CLI report success remains blocked until genuine public-read and external rights/access authority are supplied. No permissive verifier, bypass flag, hash, path or synthetic label grants authority. Private exposure/retention contracts have synthetic checks; no real issuer, protected heldout release, licensed speech corpus or independent custody/expiry authority is established. Crash/uncertainty cannot imply a refund, retry grant or unused-set status.
- **Persistence scope:** Synthetic SQLite tests and a fresh-interpreter reopen retain effects and unknown liability. They do not prove crash atomicity under every OS/filesystem fault, race-free artifact replacement, ACL isolation, cross-machine ownership or production database operation. Retention plans are bounded metadata proposals; declared hashes are not verified bytes, and no automatic deletion workflow exists.
- **K and identity evidence:** Mock K execution is distinct from licensed fixed-PCM K. No real licensed asset, rights record or approval verifier is supplied for live K. Control completion is not task/spoken correctness; known zero applies only to no-provider generation, not licensing or study costs. Admission fingerprints and parser observations are testimony, not independent provider identity or omitted-content proof. Exact run/trial/session/boot/turn/epoch lineage must remain intact.
- **Build portability and security scope:** Generated bindings include raw source hashes; Windows CRLF and canonical LF bytes can produce different reproduction pins. Preserve both and use reviewed canonical-EOL build inputs rather than weaken parity. The successful tested build and zero-vulnerability npm snapshot do not certify every checkout, browser, dependency platform or security boundary.
- **Docker scope:** The final network-disabled, non-root image executes the same two inline public-development checks from the pinned code; it is not a browser/full-soak run, hosted deployment or live-provider test. There is no publication approval or production-readiness claim.

The selected live paired comparison, independent custody and independent acceptance remain open. No hiring outcome, complete project delivery or public deployment follows from offline instrumentation evidence. No recorded demo, measured demo duration or measured latency result is supplied.
